About

Built for a better
relationship with AI.

Crown is a private AI workspace built by BlackCrown VxJ, LLC.

Most AI tools are a blank box you talk into and forget. Your conversations pile up in an endless scroll, your data may be used to train someone else's model, and you're metered by the message. We thought the everyday experience of using AI deserved better.

What we believe

  • Your work is yours. Private by default, never sold, never used to train models.
  • Organization beats chaos. Projects give your threads a home so your work stays in context.
  • Refinement matters. One focused assistant with taste, not a cluttered switchboard.
  • Fair, simple pricing. Flat and unlimited on Pro, no token anxiety.
