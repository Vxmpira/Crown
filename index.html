Questions

Straight answers.

Is the free tier actually free? +

Yes. No card, no trial clock. Standard gives you three rooms with a daily allowance that resets every day. It only costs money when you choose Crowned or buy tokens for Deep-Logic.

What happens to my data? +

Nothing you write, upload, or track in Crown is sold or used to train a model. That is the standard across every BlackCrownVxJ product, not a setting you go hunting for. The full detail lives in the privacy policy.

What are tokens and why does Deep-Logic need them? +

Deep-Logic runs a heavier reasoning model that costs real compute per answer. Crowned includes a monthly grant, and top-ups start at $4.99 inside the console. Balances never expire, and they overflow into the other rooms if your daily allowance runs dry.

How is this connected to Korvus and Eclipse-X? +

Same house, same desk. BlackCrown Intelligence is the AI division of BlackCrownVxJ.LLC, which also ships Korvus market intelligence, the LodeStone indicator, and runs the Eclipse-X community on Discord. Each stands alone; together they compound.

Can I cancel Crowned any time? +

Yes, from inside the console in two clicks. You keep Crowned until the end of the period you paid for, then land back on Standard. Any token balance you bought stays yours.
