QUESTIONS // STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Asked and answered.

What are the Four Rooms, really?

Four separately tuned AI environments inside one console. Each room runs its own engine and its own temperament: Unfiltered for directness, Deep-Logic for step-by-step reasoning, Visual Analysis for charts and screenshots, Creativity for volume and range. You pick the room, the room signs its replies, and each room keeps its own thread with you.

Is the free tier actually useful?

Yes. Standard gives you Crown's base chat with a monthly allowance, no card required. The Four Rooms, web access, image generation, uploads, and Projects are Crowned features.

What happens to my data?

Nothing you write or upload is sold or used to train a model. That is the standard across the whole BlackCrown house, not a toggle. Your chats live on your account and you can delete them, or the account, whenever you choose.

How does billing work?

Checkout and subscriptions run on Stripe. Crowned is $49.99 a month, renews monthly, and cancels anytime from your account portal in two clicks. Token top-ups for heavy months are on the way.

Where does Crown sit in the BlackCrown house?

BlackCrown is one house with several edges: Korvus for market intelligence, LodeStone on the chart, Eclipse-X for the community, and Crown as the AI division. Each stands alone; together they compound. The house itself lives at blackcrownvxj.com.
