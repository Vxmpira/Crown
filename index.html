Questions

Straight answers.

Is the free tier actually free? +

Yes. No card, no trial clock. Standard gives you the Crown console with a monthly allowance that resets every 30 days. The four rooms are Crowned territory. It only costs money when you choose Crowned or top up credit for Deep-Logic.

What happens to my data? +

Nothing you write, upload, or track in Crown is sold or used to train a model. That is the standard across every BlackCrownVxJ product, not a setting you go hunting for. The full detail lives in the privacy policy.

How does usage work? +

Usage is a money balance, metered live. Crowned carries $100 of room credit a month, and Deep-Logic draws more per answer because it runs a heavier reasoning model. Top-ups start at $4.99 inside the console, balances never expire, and they overflow into the other rooms if your allowance runs dry.

How is this connected to Korvus and Eclipse-X? +

Same house, same desk. BlackCrown Intelligence is the AI division of BlackCrownVxJ.LLC, which also ships Korvus market intelligence, the LodeStone indicator, and runs the Eclipse-X community on Discord. Each stands alone; together they compound.

Can I cancel Crowned any time? +

Yes, from inside the console in two clicks. You keep Crowned until the end of the period you paid for, then land back on Standard. Any top-up balance you bought stays yours.
